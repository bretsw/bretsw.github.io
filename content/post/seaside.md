I came across a poem from Wendell Berry that captured my imagination, and I was so happy to try create this moment at Loch Bay on the Isle of Skye, Scotland:

> Between the sky and sea  
A lamplit table spread  
Old hospitality  
Of cheese and wine and bread.

>The sea’s undying sound  
Demarked a land unknown  
To us, who therein found  
Welcome, our travel done.

~ Wendell Berry
